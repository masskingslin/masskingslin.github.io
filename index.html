Privacy Policy for Holy Bible

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Holy Bible ("the App") is developed by VedaVachanam. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account, login, or personal registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location, and the App does not access your camera, contacts, or microphone.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves your preferences (such as last-read verse, font size, and settings) locally on your device using browser local storage. This information is never transmitted to us and stays on your device unless you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob. To show relevant ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data such as advertising ID, device information, and general location (derived from IP address) in accordance with their own privacy practices. This data collection is performed by Google, not directly by us. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • How Google uses information from partner sites
  • Manage your ad settings

4. Text-to-Speech

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read verses aloud. This processing happens entirely on your device and no audio or text is sent to our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.
